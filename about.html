About DividendRadar & Our Methodology

DividendRadar was created to provide individual retail investors with straightforward, reliable, and noise-free access to daily U.S. stock market dividend schedules.

Data Sources & Trust (E-E-A-T)

All ex-dividend information displayed on DividendRadar is retrieved daily via automated feeds directly linked to official U.S. stock exchange registers. We cross-reference our schedules using authoritative financial frameworks:

  • Primary Data Feed: Extracted systematically from the Official Nasdaq Dividend Calendar.
  • Regulatory Compliance: Our explanations regarding Ex-Dividend and Record Dates align strictly with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) guidelines.
  • External Verification: Every ticker on our tables links directly to external validation platforms (like Yahoo Finance) so you can verify payout ratios and balance sheets instantly.

Editorial & Technical Team

This platform is maintained by a dedicated team of quantitative developers and retail dividend investors. Our system runs a strict Python-based cron job every morning before the New York trading session opens, ensuring data freshness and removing human-entry errors from the equation.
